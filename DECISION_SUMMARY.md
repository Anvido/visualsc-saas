# VISUALSC: 4-Week MVP vs 24-Week MVP - Decision Matrix

## One Page Summary

### The Question
**Should we build a lean 4-week validation MVP or a full 24-week production MVP?**

### The Trade-off

| Dimension | 4-Week MVP | 24-Week MVP |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Time to Market** | 4 weeks | 24 weeks |
| **Budget** | $15.5K | $120K-150K |
| **Team Size** | 2-3 people | 5-7 people |
| **Code Quality** | Good enough | Production grade |
| **Scalability** | Limited (3 restaurants) | Unlimited (1000+) |
| **Manual Work** | 50% of operations | Fully automated |
| **Risk Level** | Low (learn fast) | High (build wrong thing) |
| **Validation** | Which product to build | Building perfect product |

---

## The Argument for 4-Week MVP ✅

### **You should do the 4-week MVP if:**

- ✅ **Unsure if deaf customers will actually use digital menus** (biggest risk)
- ✅ **Unsure if restaurants will pay** (second biggest risk)
- ✅ **Want to prove concept before raising capital**
- ✅ **Have limited runway** (< $50K)
- ✅ **Need to learn fast** (market moves quickly in Colombia)
- ✅ **Willing to manually support early customers** (concierge model)
- ✅ **Want to de-risk before hiring team**

### **What you prove in 4 weeks:**
1. Deaf customers choose LSC menu > 30% of time
2. Restaurants use system daily > 80%
3. Restaurants willing to pay ($50-100/month)
4. Orders execute without major issues
5. Business model might work at scale

### **Then you decide:**
- ✅ **Go build 24-week MVP** (all cylinders, proper product)
- 🔄 **Pivot market segment** (B2B instead of B2C, etc.)
- 🔄 **Pivot feature set** (no LSC, just menu management)
- ❌ **Stop and try something else** (market doesn't exist)

---

## The Argument for 24-Week MVP ❌ (Not Recommended)

### **You should skip 4-week MVP only if:**

- ✅ Capital is not a constraint (already funded)
- ✅ Time is critical (someone beating you to market)
- ✅ You have deaf SMEs on team (can validate internally)
- ✅ You're replacing legacy system at known customer
- ✅ Your instincts are 100% certain about market

### **Risk:**
- ❌ Spend $120K and 6 months on wrong product
- ❌ Deaf community doesn't adopt (cultural mismatch)
- ❌ Restaurants need concierge, not SaaS (scaling nightmare)
- ❌ Business model breaks at small scale
- ❌ Burned out team before launch

---

## My Recommendation: **Do the 4-Week MVP First**

### **Reasoning:**

1. **Biggest risk is unvalidated:** You don't know if deaf customers will use this. This is a market assumption, not a product assumption. Test it cheaply.

2. **Concierge is acceptable:** You can support 3 restaurants manually. This teaches you what needs to be automated and what can stay manual.

3. **Fast learning:** 4 weeks of real usage > 24 weeks of guessing. You'll learn more in month 1 live than in month 5 of development.

4. **Low sunk cost:** If it fails, you've lost $15.5K and 4 weeks, not $120K and 6 months.

5. **Founder learning:** Managing 3 restaurants live teaches you more about the business than any planning can.

6. **Capital efficiency:** Use 4 weeks to prove concept, then raise seed round with real data (3 paying restaurants, usage metrics).

7. **Team motivation:** Launch something real (even if rough) > Ship perfect thing no one uses.

## Decision Framework: When to Switch to 24-Week MVP

### You're ready to switch to full development when:

1. ✅ **3 restaurants have 30%+ LSC adoption** (market signal)
2. ✅ **80%+ daily usage rate** (adoption signal)
3. ✅ **All willing to pay $50-100/month** (commercial signal)
4. ✅ **100+ orders placed successfully** (reliability signal)
5. ✅ **Deaf community positive feedback** (mission signal)

### You're NOT ready if:

1. ❌ LSC adoption < 10% (UX/market problem)
2. ❌ Restaurant usage < 50% daily (GTM problem)
3. ❌ No willingness to pay (economic problem)
4. ❌ Support burden > 5 hours/week per restaurant (scalability problem)
5. ❌ Deaf community concerns (mission problem)

**If any red flag appears → Pivot product, not roadmap.**

## Risk Analysis: What Can Go Wrong?

### Biggest Risks in Priority Order

1. **Deaf customers won't use LSC menu** (Market Risk)
   - Impact: HIGH (product is irrelevant)
   - Likelihood: MEDIUM (cultural assumptions)
   - Test by: Week 2 (visit restaurants, observe real deaf users)

2. **Restaurants won't engage daily** (Adoption Risk)
   - Impact: HIGH (can't scale)
   - Likelihood: MEDIUM (technology skepticism)
   - Test by: Week 3 (login tracking, update frequency)

3. **Manual operations are bottleneck** (Scalability Risk)
   - Impact: MEDIUM (restricts growth)
   - Likelihood: HIGH (certain to happen, but can address)
   - Mitigate: Identify what must be automated early

4. **Willingness to pay is low** (Economic Risk)
   - Impact: HIGH (business model breaks)
   - Likelihood: LOW (SaaS model is proven elsewhere)
   - Test by: Week 2 (ask directly, not end of month 1)

5. **Orders get lost or mishandled** (Reliability Risk)
   - Impact: MEDIUM (one bad incident = churn)
   - Likelihood: MEDIUM (complex flow)
   - Mitigate: Redundant notifications, paper backup

## Go/No-Go Decision at Week 4

### Green Light Scenario ✅

All of these are true:
- [ ] 30%+ of customers chose LSC menu
- [ ] 80%+ of restaurants logging in daily
- [ ] 3/3 restaurants willing to pay
- [ ] 50+ orders placed with no lost orders
- [ ] Deaf community positive feedback

**Decision: Raise seed capital ($200-300K), build 24-week MVP, expand to 10+ restaurants**

Some bright spots, some concerns:
- [ ] LSC adoption lower than hoped, but shows promise
- [ ] Restaurant adoption good but needs training
- [ ] Mixed on payment (1/3 hesitant)
- [ ] Operations smoother than expected

**Decision: Keep operating 3 restaurants, refine model, try 1-2 more, then decide**

### Red Light Scenario ❌

One or more failed:
- [ ] < 10% chose LSC menu (wrong product)
- [ ] < 50% daily adoption (wrong GTM)
- [ ] 0/3 willing to pay (wrong economics)
- [ ] Lost orders or major issues (wrong execution)

**Decision: Pivot product or GTM, or stop**
